## Shared visual language observed

Black/dark backgrounds (`#000`, `#0d0f14`, `#0a0a0a`), white and neutral text, amber/white emphasis, occasional purple for concessions, thin translucent white borders, square-to-small-radius controls, uppercase micro-labels with letter spacing, serif/italic display movie headings paired with sans body text, poster aspect ratios close to 2:3, hero banners close to 16:9, lucide icons, hover/focus transitions, dark overlays/backdrop blur for drawers/modals.

## Customer-only exclusions

`/admin/*`, `/manager/*`, `/staff`, staff/admin/manager pages, dashboards, inventory and operational panels are excluded from this inventory.
